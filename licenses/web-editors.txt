Third-party JavaScript bundled in the autophys web app
================================================================================
html/routes/js/codemirror.min.js and html/routes/js/prosemirror.min.js are
prebuilt browser bundles generated by tento-plugins-js (codemirror/ and
prosemirror/) from the npm packages listed here. Their notices follow, as
shipped in each package. Type-only packages (@types/*) and markdown-it's
command-line dependency (argparse) are not part of either bundle.
